Chickovia Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chickovia (the “Application”) handles information. The Application is operated by Orik Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

Platforms covered

This policy covers the Android version and any iOS version that may be released. Platform-specific services and controls apply only when included in the version installed on your device.

Information provided directly to Orik Studio

The Application does not require an account or registration. Normal gameplay does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, date of birth, or precise location. The Application does not provide online chat or public player profiles.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond, provide support, handle privacy requests, protect our rights, or comply with law.

Information stored on your device

The Application stores game progress and preferences locally on your device. This includes chickens and collections, virtual coins, upgrades, tasks, farm names you choose, game-mode progress, sound and language settings, tutorial state, and records needed to apply advertising rewards and temporary bonuses.

Orik Studio does not operate an account database or a cloud-save server for this game. Local game saves are not uploaded to Orik Studio. Device or operating-system backup services, if enabled, may separately retain application data under their own settings and policies.

Advertising and third-party services

The Application uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Watching a rewarded advertisement may provide an in-game benefit, such as a farm unlock, a move undo, a temporary income bonus, or a temporary pause of automatic interstitial advertisements.

AdMob may collect and share technical and advertising information with Google and advertising partners. Depending on the platform, SDK version, device settings, and applicable choices, this may include:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • advertising identifiers and other device, app, or account-related identifiers available to the SDK;
  • app and advertisement interactions, including launches, taps, impressions, and video views; and
  • diagnostic information, crash information, and performance information such as launch time, hangs, and energy usage.

This information is used to deliver and measure advertisements, perform advertising analytics, maintain service reliability and security, and prevent fraud and invalid ad activity. Google describes its practices in the Google Privacy Policy, advertising privacy information, and partner-app information.

The Application does not include a separate Orik Studio analytics service, online leaderboard, platform-account sign-in, or real-money in-app purchasing system. Purchases made with virtual game coins are local gameplay transactions.

Android and iOS privacy controls

Android: AdMob may use the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other identifiers available to the SDK. You can manage, reset, or delete your advertising ID through the advertising or privacy controls in your Android device settings. Available options depend on the Android version and device.

iOS: AdMob may use app- or developer-scoped identifiers and, when permitted by your device’s authorization, the advertising identifier (IDFA). You can review tracking permissions in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Access to IDFA is subject to Apple’s App Tracking Transparency requirements. Refusing tracking permission does not itself remove advertisements or all technical processing by an advertising service.

Where a released version provides a consent or privacy-choice interface, you can use that interface to manage the choices it offers. Device controls and the provider’s privacy tools offer additional options. Availability of personalized advertising and privacy choices depends on the region, platform, and version. This policy does not replace any consent required by applicable law.

Downloading the Application or using device backup and store services may involve separate processing by Google or Apple. See the Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Permissions and location

The Application uses network access for advertising and can use device vibration for optional gameplay feedback. Normal gameplay does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location. An advertising service may infer approximate location from an IP address without accessing GPS.

Why information is processed

  • to save gameplay progress and preferences on your device;
  • to display and measure advertisements and apply in-game rewards;
  • to prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity;
  • to maintain security, reliability, and performance;
  • to answer support and privacy requests; and
  • to comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, the relevant legal basis depends on the processing: providing requested game and support functions, consent where required for advertising-related processing, legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Third-party providers describe the legal bases for processing they control in their own policies.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent using the applicable privacy-choice interface or provider controls. You may also contact us about processing directly controlled by Orik Studio. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Sharing and sale

Orik Studio does not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Advertising information may be transmitted to Google and advertising partners through AdMob for the purposes described above. Some advertising disclosures may be considered “sale” or “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising under certain United States laws.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider. We may disclose information under our control when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, protect users or the public, or investigate fraud or security incidents.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is overwritten or removed. On Android, you can clear the Application’s storage through device settings or uninstall it. On iOS, deleting the Application normally removes its local data; offloading it may preserve data. Operating-system backups may retain copies separately.

If you contact Orik Studio by email, correspondence may remain in the email account for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

You may request deletion of personal data directly controlled by Orik Studio, such as support correspondence, by emailing orxanb121@mail.ru. Retention may be required or permitted by applicable law.

Google, Apple, and other providers retain information they control according to their own privacy policies. Uninstalling the Application or clearing a local save does not delete information already processed by those services. Orik Studio cannot directly delete information controlled solely by a third-party provider.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, to withdraw consent, or to complain to a data-protection authority. For requests concerning personal data directly controlled by Orik Studio, contact orxanb121@mail.ru. We may need proportionate information to verify your request.

Requests concerning information controlled solely by Google or Apple should be submitted using that provider’s privacy controls, account settings, or support channels. Android advertising-ID controls and iOS tracking controls can limit certain identifier uses; they do not necessarily stop all processing needed to deliver advertisements.

Children’s privacy

Orik Studio does not knowingly request personal information directly from children through a Chickovia account or registration form, because the Application has neither. Advertising SDKs may still process technical information as described above; the absence of registration does not mean that advertising involves no data processing.

Users below the age at which they may legally consent to relevant processing in their country should use the Application only with a parent’s or guardian’s authorization where required by law. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to Orik Studio or have a concern about a child’s privacy, contact us so we can review the issue and take appropriate action.

International transfers and security

Orik Studio is based in Azerbaijan. Our email provider, Google, Apple, and advertising partners may process information in other countries. Their privacy policies explain their transfer safeguards. Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy when the Application, its SDKs, supported platforms, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same URL with a revised effective date.
